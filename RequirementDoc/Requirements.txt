

**Vertex WM – Smart Employee & Internship Management Platform**

VertexWM (appname) is a comprehensive workforce management platform designed to simplify employee and internship operations through a structured, role-based management system. Built for organizations of all sizes, Vertex enables administrators to efficiently manage teams while giving leadership complete visibility into workforce performance and productivity.

### Core Features

* **Employee Management**

  * Maintain employee profiles and departments
  * Manage onboarding, roles, and permissions
  * Track employment status and organizational hierarchy

* **Intern Management**

  * Organize internship batches and programs
  * Assign mentors and managers
  * Monitor intern progress, performance, and completion

* **Role-Based Access Control**

  * **Super Admin (Managing Director):** Full organizational oversight, analytics, and reporting
  * **Admin (Managers):** Manage employees, interns, attendance, tasks, and approvals
  * **Employees & Interns:** Update attendance, submit timesheets, complete assigned tasks, and report daily progress

* **Task Management** with Project Management

  * Create and assign daily or project-based tasks
  * Set priorities, deadlines, and progress tracking
  * Monitor task completion and team performance

* **Daily Attendance**

  * Clock-in and clock-out functionality
  * Attendance history and reports
  * Late arrivals, absences, and work-hour tracking

* **Timesheet Management**

  * Record daily working hours
  * Log time spent on projects and tasks
  * Manager approval workflow and reporting

* **Daily Work Updates**

  * Employees and interns submit end-of-day reports
  * Managers review progress and provide feedback
  * Centralized activity history for transparency

* **Productivity Analytics**

  * Individual and team productivity dashboards
  * Attendance and task completion metrics
  * Timesheet utilization analysis
  * Performance insights and trend reports

* **Reports & Monitoring**

  * Real-time dashboards for managers and executives
  * Attendance, productivity, and task completion reports
  * Exportable summaries for business insights

### Why Vertex?

Vertex centralizes workforce management by combining employee administration, internship management, attendance tracking, timesheets, task management, and productivity analytics into a single platform. With role-based access and real-time monitoring, managers can efficiently oversee daily operations while leadership gains clear visibility into organizational performance, ensuring accountability, collaboration, and continuous productivity improvement.
